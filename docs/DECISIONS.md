# Decisions log (build)

Product decisions live in `BRIEF.md` §8 and `../design/HANDOFF.md` §3. Log here every decision taken while building, and every deviation from the design, newest first.

| Date | Decision | Reason | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-15 | Rules are **soft** for City, E-bike, Road, Gravel, MTB (Best = lowest time + rule penalty, never blocked; what remains is shown on the route) and **strict** for Cargo / kids (quickest route meeting every rule, else *No route meets your rules* naming the rule) | The design's City Best keeps 70 m of cobbles at Piazza Ascoli with *No cobbles* on, while its Cargo case is blocked; strict rules blocked most Milan trips for every type | Claude |
| 2026-10-15 | Rule penalties (seconds of detour per metre): cobbles 1, rails along 3, steps/narrow gaps 3, unpaved 0.7, steep 2, mixed traffic 0.5, unlit 0.7 | Cobbles ≈ 1 s/m reproduces the design's "+5 min · avoids 300 m cobbles" | Claude |
| 2026-10-15 | Pack retags cobbled ways `surface=dirt` for routing (original in `velix:surface`); `avoid_bad_surfaces` follows the *No cobbles* / *Paved roads only* rules; Cargo / kids uses Valhalla's road-bike surface model | Valhalla files `sett` as plain paved (like asphalt), so its costing never avoided cobbles; native costing is fast where exclusion polygons are not | Claude |
| 2026-10-15 | Exclusion polygons only reactively, ≤ 60 per plan, one trimmed box per edge (8 m short of each junction), early stop, 1.2 s budget (2.4 s Cargo) | valhalla-mobile 0.6.3 slows sharply with many polygons (13–27 s for 100–200 KB of boxes on an M1); boxes over junctions also removed cross streets | Claude |
| 2026-10-15 | Elevation: GEDTM30 (global 30 m terrain model, CC BY 4.0) instead of Copernicus GLO-30; slopes from a profile smoothed over ±250 m and measured on 300 m windows | GLO-30 is a surface model (roofs): flat Milan streets read 8–15 %. Even GEDTM30 keeps ±15–25 m bumps in dense blocks; 300 m windows keep Milan ≤ 5 % and Bergamo Alta at 7.3 %. Short steep ramps (< 300 m) aren't detected; revisit with a finer DTM | Claude |
| 2026-10-15 | *Bike lanes only*: lanes, tracks, paths and quiet streets (residential, living street, or any road ≤ 30 km/h); mixed traffic allowed only ≤ 150 m at a time | Universal (Zona 30 / 20 mph zones); a strict lanes-only reading blocks almost every trip | Claude |
| 2026-10-15 | *Never along tram rails* ignores rails beside a separated track or path; *No steps or narrow gaps* counts cycle barriers, kissing gates, stiles, turnstiles and maxwidth < 1 m, not bollards or open gates | Rails behind a kerb aren't the hazard; Lombardy has 65k barriers, mostly bollards | Claude |
| 2026-10-15 | First and last 50 m of a route don't count against rules | Your own doorstep may be cobbled | Claude |

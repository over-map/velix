VELIX · P8 · ACCESSIBILITY AND EDGE STATES · 11 OCT 2026

Readable large, legible without colour, spoken clearly.

Dynamic Type XL for S3, S4 and S8; colour-blind simulation of S3 and the riding map; VoiceOver labels for every control and card; the fixes applied. Frames are the real P4, P5 and P7 screens re-rendered at the larger size or through the filter.

01 · RESULT

Findings and fixes

FixedS4 · maneuver bannerAt 115% “Then ↱ P.za Adelaide” was cut off by the road-type pill. The row now wraps: the pill drops to its own line instead of truncating the street. Applied to every riding screen (P5, P6).
FixedS3 · map above the sheetAt 135% the sheet rises by about 150 pt. The map now refits the route into the space left above it, so start, end and hazards stay visible.
CheckedS3 · route cardsNames, times and difference lines reflow; times ≥ 30 pt don’t scale. Nothing truncates on the Politecnico and Monza cases.
CheckedS8 · rowsRule rows grow to two lines and switches stay centred. The full page scrolls; no row truncates.
CheckedCVD · route vs hazardIn deuteranopia and protanopia the coral hazard and the chartreuse route both read as yellow. They stay apart by lightness, the ink casing on the route, the dark ring and glyph on hazard markers and the striped tube. No colour change.
CheckedCVD · mix barProtected, quiet and mixed come close in protanopia. Painted (ticks), cobbles (hatch) and unpaved (dots) carry patterns, and the difference line names the surface in words, so the bar is never the only signal.
CheckedCVD · avoided and EndLilac avoided roads turn blue but stay dashed with a ✕ tag and the reason. The red End button turns olive, near the hazard card: different place, label and shape, so it stays clear.
SpecVoiceOver · mapMap lines are not focusable. Every line has an accessible twin: route cards, hazard rows, numbered route keys (table below).
SpecReduce MotionNo animated tilt into 3D, recenter jumps instead of flying, the rerouting spinner becomes a static icon.
OpenHapticsThe two-tap hazard buzz is the only cue that is neither visual nor audio. Engineering to confirm it stays distinct on a handlebar mount.

VELIX · P4 · RIDE, SEARCH, ROUTE OPTIONS · 9 OCT 2026

Open, Work, Start. Two taps to ride.

S1 Ride, its two sheets, S2 Search and S3 Route options at iPhone 15 size (393 × 852), light and dark. Every map is drawn with the P3 v3 Chart renderer from real OpenFreeMap tiles and real bike routes. Components come from P2 v2.

Commute = 2 tapsOpen the app, tap Work (S1a), tap Start (S3a). No ride-type or route decision in between: Best is preselected.
Ride type proves itselfThe same Politecnico trip as City (S3a), Cargo / kids (S3i) and Gravel (S3j): new Best name, new mix, Outdoor map for Gravel.
Sample dataTimes, distances and the Trenord timetable are the brief’s sample data, illustrative. Map lines are real bike routing on OSM.

03 · OUTDOOR · GRAVEL, MTB, E-BIKE

Outdoor: Parco di Monza

Adds Copernicus/SRTM hillshade (4× exaggeration, since Monza only has about 50 m of relief), 5 m contours with 25 m index lines, tree texture in woods, and track surfaces: paved solid, unpaved dashed, paths dotted. The loop is a real bike-routed 8.6 km from Villa Reale via Viale Cavriga.

06 · METRO VS ACCENT

Yellow metro never reads as your route

Chartreuse #DBF227 and metro yellow #F5C400 are different hues, and form still does the work. The route is at least 3.5× wider and heavily cased. Metro lines are hairline-cased, have station tiles with line numbers, sit at 60% opacity under an active route, and draw dashed with a badge when ridden.

07 · METRO COLOURS ACROSS EUROPE

12 colour families, not 200 brand hexes

Velix runs in every European city, so metro lines don't use each operator's exact hex. A line's OSM colour=* is converted to OKLCH and snapped to one of 12 Velix families by hue and lightness. Red stays red, green stays green, and every family is tuned once for contrast and kept apart from the accent, hazard and avoided colours. When two lines in one city land in the same family, the second gets the alt tone. A third reuses the main tone, and the line number on station tiles tells them apart.

Snapping, from real OSM colours
Rule. grey if chroma < 0.04 · brown if hue 30–95° and L < 0.6 · red/pink/purple around 330–30° by lightness · orange 30–70° · yellow or olive 70–130° by lightness · green 130–165° · cyan 165–215° · light blue or blue 215–275° by lightness · purple 275–330°.
Signals win. Purple sits on the red side of violet so it never reads as avoided lilac. Green is bluer than the lime protected lane. Red is a cool crimson, away from hazard coral. Form does the rest: metro is 2.5 pt, cased, with station tiles.

08 · EUROVELO

One badge for all 17 EuroVelo routes

EuroVelo's own panel (Council of Europe blue with stars) is a registered mark, and the ECF has to approve logo use. So Velix uses its own badge: navy tile, khaki ring, Lucide bike, “EV” and the number. One design for every route, no per-route colours. On the map the route is a soft ribbon under the cycle infrastructure, with badges along it. Lines come from OSM network=icn relations. The route list is checked against the ECF once a year (EV16 and EV21 are planned).

09 · PALETTE

Basemap ramps

The basemap sits in a narrow band so nothing in it competes with navigation colours. Night is the reference mood. Day is the same chart printed on paper.

10 · CONTRAST

Line contrast against each basemap

WCAG ratio of the better of fill or casing/halo against the land colour, plus the worst case against the street fill: white by day, navy #133F5A at night. Khaki hairlines are 0.3–1.4 pt wide and lines draw over them, so they're never the background a line is read against. Target ≥ 3:1.

11 · LAYER SPEC

Layer by layer, bottom to top

Source omt is OpenMapTiles from OpenFreeMap. Velix sources cover what OMT lacks: velix_cycling (on-road lanes), velix_transit (metro lines and colours), velix_landmarks (curated landmark ids and names), velix_shadows (precomputed building shadows, because MapLibre has no shadow casting), and velix_dem. Widths are pt at MapLibre zooms 13 · 15 · 17, interpolated exponentially (base 1.5).

12 · LABELS AND POIS

Labels

Fonts. Atkinson Hyperlegible Regular and Bold, as self-hosted SDF glyphs. Familjen Grotesk is never used on the map.
Priority (symbol-sort-key, low wins): 1 hazards and avoided tags, 2 start and end, 3 landmarks, 4 metro and rail stations, 4.5 EuroVelo badges, 5 places, 6 road names, 7 POIs, 8 house numbers.
Roads. Placed along the line. Primary from z14.6, all classes from z15.2, paths from z16. 11 / 12 / 13 pt at z15 / 16 / 17. Primary names are Bold. Road labels draw below the route layer, so the route covers its own street names. The UI carries those.
Places. Suburbs in caps, Bold 12 pt, +0.08 em, z11.5–15.6. Neighbourhoods Bold 10.5 pt, z13.8–16.4.
Landmarks. Bold 12 pt in landmark ink, anchored to the roof, from z14.4.
POIs. 13 pt category discs in muted tones (food, shop, education, health, culture, worship, lodging, green, water). Bike parking uses mix.protected. Civic categories show from z15.6, everything from z16.4, with names from z16.8.
House numbers from z17.6, 9.5 pt, at the lowest priority.

01

02
Contrast
Computed from the tokens above. Text needs 4.5:1; map lines 3:1 against the map. Chart direction: chartreuse carries 14.7:1 with abyss text. Hazard coral and route-alt slate clear 3:1 on the paper map. Hazard-ink is near-black because white on coral fails.
